3 · FM reporting standard — metric definitions

Governance reference

One calculation, one source, one owner per metric — computed centrally, then sliced by region, facility type, and site. CRV is updated annually for inflation; gross square footage comes from the space system of record; energy is weather-normalized and type-segmented via ENERGY STAR; FCI is always sourced from a formal condition assessment, never a raw work-order backlog. RAG = green (on target) / amber (watch) / red (action). Thresholds are starting points to ratify; owners are role placeholders.
